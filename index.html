Steam › Account › Spending replay

    Replay everything you've spent on Steam

    Save a couple of pages from your Steam account, drop them here, and get a breakdown of every purchase, sale, gift and key since you signed up.

    1. Save your purchase history

      Open store.steampowered.com/account/history while signed in. Scroll to the bottom and click Load more transactions until the button goes away. Then press Ctrl+S (⌘+S on a Mac) and save it as Webpage, Complete.

    2. Save your licenses optional, adds product keys, free games and gifts with their dates

      Open store.steampowered.com/account/licenses and save it the same way. Steam splits your licenses into pages of about 100, so save every page (use the page numbers at the bottom) and drop them all in together.

    3. Save your games page optional, adds your real game count and playtime

      Open steamcommunity.com/my/games, wait for the list to finish loading, and save it the same way. That saved page also contains a short-lived sign-in token from Steam. This page never reads or sends it anywhere, but don't share that file with anyone.

    4. Drop the .html files here

      Only the .html files are needed; the folders saved next to them can be ignored. Steam needs to be set to English.

    Everything is worked out in your browser. Your files are never uploaded or stored anywhere.
